# 💫 About Me

I'm a data-driven **Pricing Manager** with a strong foundation in business analytics, currently pursuing a Business Finance Diploma at Fanshawe College. My career has been shaped by a deep passion for turning complex data into actionable strategy - from predictive modelling and sales forecasting to pricing optimization and cross-functional collaboration.

Over four years as a Senior Data Analyst at Bartle & Gibson, I led a team in building automated workflows and predictive models using SQL, Power BI, and Excel, while mentoring junior analysts and driving measurable improvements in reporting efficiency. Now in my Pricing Manager role, I apply that same analytical mindset to develop data-backed pricing strategies that directly impact business performance.

I hold several Microsoft certifications - including **Power BI Data Analyst**, **Power Platform**, and **Azure Data Fundamentals** - and I'm continuously expanding my toolkit with modern data science and deployment technologies. My long-term goal is to grow into a Data Scientist role, leveraging machine learning and AI to solve high-impact business challenges.
